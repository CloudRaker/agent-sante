when to use epinephrine (e.g., epipen, allerject or emerade); when to to go to the hospital right away. see the anaphylactic shock advice page in case of a very severe allergic reaction. avoid avoid the food that causes the allergy or intolerance. avoid foods sold in bulk. do not share food or utensils. do not cook different foods in the same oil. when to seek medical attention official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/food-allergies-or-intolerances/ the same symptoms occur every time after eating or touching the same food: skin redness nausea or vomiting stomach aches diarrhea (watery stools) need help? call info-santé at 811, option 1, to speak with a nurse at any time. go to the pharmacy for advice on medications or treatment. make an appointment at your medical clinic or clsc. if you do not have a family doctor, go to gap.soinsvirtuels or call 811, option 3. when to go to the hospital right away official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/food-allergies-or-intolerances/ after receiving epinephrine (e.g., epipen, allerject or emerade), even if symptoms improve or go away after contact with a food that has previously caused a severe allergic reaction symptoms of a severe allergic reaction (redness, itchy skin, or swelling of the lips, tongue or face), along with at least one of the following: a high-pitched sound when breathing (stridor) or difficulty breathing bluish or purplish lips or fingers feeling weak or dizzy, or loss of consciousness a very rapid heartbeat stomach ache, vomiting or diarrhea (watery stools) for young children (ages 0 to 2): not responding as usual, being limp or difficult to wake
