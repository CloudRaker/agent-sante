about (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry/description) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry/description the québec vaccination registry is an electronic database designed to provide health network professionals (e.g., doctors, nurses) with access to information on vaccines received by individuals in québec. the registry has been gradually introduced in all regions of québec from june 2014 to december 2018. see also vaccination for children understanding vaccines last update: april 20, 2018
