headache or migraine official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/headache-or-migraine/ what it is official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/headache-or-migraine/ a headache is pain felt in the head or face. it often goes away after a few hours. a migraine is a headache that comes back often and may be more severe. it can make everyday activities more difficult. what you can do at home official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/headache-or-migraine/ what to do to relieve pain, take acetaminophen (tylenol) or ibuprofen (advil). if in doubt, follow the advice provided by a pharmacist. important: ibuprofen is contraindicated for children under 6 months of age. if you have a prescription for migraine medication, take it as directed when symptoms start. ask a pharmacist for advice if the medication does not work. other treatment options may be suggested. apply cold apply cold to the forehead for 20 minutes every 2 hours, as needed. use a cold compress, an ice pack, a bag of frozen vegetables or a cold gel pack. always place a cloth between the skin and the cold source to protect the skin. rest and relieve tension lie down in a dark, quiet room with your eyes closed. wear sunglasses if light is bothersome. stretch and massage the neck if the muscles are tense. relax by breathing slowly or listening to soft music. avoid avoid drinking alcohol. avoid using drugs. reduce exposure to light. when to seek medical attention official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/headache-or-migraine/ a headache that does not improve or gets worse after 24 hours, even after following the advice provided a headache that feels different from usual headaches a headache that prevents usual activities, work or rest a headache that becomes more severe or headaches that occur more often a new or unusual headache in a person who: is aged 50 or older has had cancer has a weakened immune system (is immunosuppressed) need help?
