over the years, hereditary motor sensory neuropathy can lead to the following complications in affected people: it becomes increasingly difficult for them to walk, because of their muscle weakness. in adolescence, a wheelchair is needed to get around; deformity of the hands and feet, as well as the spine (scoliosis); more or less serious psychiatric problems. nearly half of those affected have psychiatric problems in adolescence and adulthood; dependence on other people, who must help the affected person carry out most activities of daily living. transmission official source:
