adapted tricycles and bicycles program (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/adapted-tricycles-and-bicycles-program) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/adapted-tricycles-and-bicycles-program description this program allows for the allocation of tricycles and bicycles suitable for people age 18 and under with disabilities. presentation and objectives allocating tricycles and bicycles adapted to minors with disabilities helps promote their social integration, skills development, and autonomy. before allocating an adapted tricycle or modifying a tricycle or a bicycle, an overall assessment of the person's needs and resources must be carried out. an adapted tricycle or bicycle is a device specially designed to meet the needs of the user. eligibility to be eligible for this program, you must: have a physical or intellectual impairments causing persistent and significant disabilities; be a minor; be unable to use a standard tricycle or bicycle; be able to pedal and steer the adapted tricycle or bicycle on your own. you are not eligible if you: already benefit from the same type of assistance provided by another national or federal program or a program offered by private insurers; live in a residential long-term care centre or a public intermediate resource. reimbursable expenses technical assistance funded under this program is provided to the user. however, a lump sum of $150 must be paid by the parent for the purchase of a tricycle, an adapted bike, or a handbike. for individuals with private insurance coverage, this program can be used to pay the uncovered portion of the costs. allocated equipment must be insured. it must be returned when the person is no longer using it or if he or she dies. process to enrol in this program, contact your territorial establishment. for contact information, consult the following page: find your health and social services facility. you will need to provide a medical certificate relating to the impairment. help and information for assistance, contact the office des personnes handicapées du québec. see also assistive devices for disability or asd last update: may 9, 2023
