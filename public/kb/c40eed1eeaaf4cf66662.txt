you must select "schedule an appointment in a clinic close to me." the search criteria are the date, reason for the consultation, postal code and search perimeter. the distance in kilometres is as the crow flies, i.e. not driving distance. as a result, the distance to get to the clinic could be greater than the distance indicated. if you are not satisfied with any of the results, refer to the répertoire des ressources en santé et services sociaux. the link is on the bottom of the calendar displaying appointments close to you. please note that you cannot schedule an appointment online with this option. you have a family doctor québec medical appointment scheduler first displays your assigned health professional's available time slots. check whether the time slots offered are suitable for you. if none of the appointment times offered is convenient, you can contact your clinic to schedule an appointment or click on "schedule an appointment with another health professional of my family medicine group (gmf)" if your clinic is part of such a group. another health professional of the group may be available at your usual clinic or at another clinic. if the appointment times offered are still not convenient, you can contact your clinic to schedule an appointment or click on "schedule an appointment in a clinic close to me." the search criteria are the date, reason for the consultation, postal code and search perimeter. the distance in kilometres is as the crow flies, i.e. not driving distance. as a result, the distance to get to the clinic could be greater than the distance indicated. if you are not satisfied with any of the results, you can contact your clinic directly. you can also refer to the répertoire des ressources en santé et services sociaux . the link
