eligibility for living kidney donation (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/blood-tissue-and-organ-donation/living-kidney-donation/eligibility) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/blood-tissue-and-organ-donation/living-kidney-donation/eligibility anyone can decide to donate a kidney. however, they must: be 18 years of age or older; be able to provide free and informed consent; meet various criteria with respect to their physical and psychosocial health. some health problems are a contraindication to kidney donation: serious heart disease; active or recent cancer; diabetes; kidney disease and kidney failure. there is no age limit for living kidney donation if there are no medical contraindications. last update: december 19, 2019
