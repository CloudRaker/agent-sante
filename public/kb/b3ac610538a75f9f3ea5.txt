official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/daily-living-and-domestic-assistance-program to be eligible for the program, the person must: reside in quebec and at the address reported as their place of residence; have a physical, organic or significant and persistent intellectual disability or autism spectrum disorder; receive assistance from a territorial establishment. a person is not eligible if he or she: already benefits from coverage from another national or federal program or a private insurance program for the same type of assistance; resides in a long-term care facility, intermediate residence (ir) or private centre. reimbursable expenses official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/daily-living-and-domestic-assistance-program there is no fee or deductible to pay, except for the self-lifting recliner. if the person benefits from private insurance coverage, this program can pay the uncovered costs. the person who receives the equipment must have it insured. it must be returned when the person is no longer using it or if the person dies. if you receive benefits from the ministère du travail, de l'emploi et de la solidarité sociale, you could get a special benefit to obtain this assistance. process official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/daily-living-and-domestic-assistance-program to enrol in this program, contact your territorial establishment. for contact information, consult the following page: find your health and social services facility. you will need to provide a medical
