reduce pain, take acetaminophen (tylenol) or ibuprofen (advil). when in doubt, ask a pharmacist for advice. important: ibuprofen is contraindicated for children under 6 months of age. if the wound is bleeding heavily cover the wound with a bandage or clean cloth. apply pressure for 15 minutes. once the bleeding has slowed, gently clean the wound. if there is debris or dirt in the wound use a compress soaked in saline solution (store-bought or homemade), or running tap water, to remove debris from the wound. to prepare a homemade saline solution: mix 240 ml (8 oz) of drinking water or boiled water. add 2.5 ml (1⁄2 teaspoon) of salt. once the wound is clear of debris, gently clean the wound. cleaning the wound clean the wound with tap water and mild, unscented soap, or with a saline solution purchased from a pharmacy or prepared at home, for a few minutes, then rinse. if a flap of skin remains attached, carefully reposition it over the wound. cover the wound with a bandage. change the bandage once a day, or sooner if it becomes wet or dirty. when to seek medical attention official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/superficial-wound/ seek medical care within
