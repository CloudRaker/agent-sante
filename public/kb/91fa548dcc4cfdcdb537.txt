source: https://sante.quebec/en/help-and-resources/mental-health-social-services/understand-mental-health/ a few guidelines to help ### approach the topic calmly choose a quiet moment. share what you've noticed without judgment: "i've noticed that you seem tired lately. i wanted to check in and see how you're doing." ### listen without trying to fix things resist the urge to give advice or minimize their suffering. "i'm here for you" can sometimes mean more than ten solutions. ask open-ended questions. ### encourage them to seek help, without pressure gently suggest speaking with a professional. offer to look for resources together or accompany them to an appointment. ### take care of yourself, too you can't fully support someone else without taking care of yourself. it's okay to seek support for yourself when helping someone who is struggling. phrases to avoid ### "just snap out of it." this minimizes their suffering by wrongly suggesting that it's simply a matter of willpower. ### "it could be worse." comparing someone's struggles can invalidate their pain and may discourage them from opening up. ### "you have everything to be happy." this overlooks the reality of mental illness and can create a heavy sense of guilt.
