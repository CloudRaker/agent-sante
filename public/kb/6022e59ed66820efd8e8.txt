anaphylactic shock (https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/anaphylactic-shock/) official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/anaphylactic-shock/ anaphylaxis what it is anaphylaxis is a very severe allergic reaction. it often occurs quickly, within minutes, but can sometimes occur up to 2 hours after touching or eating something that causes the allergy (an allergen). related sheets available: allergy, food allergies or intolerances signs of anaphylaxis symptoms of a severe allergic reaction (redness, itchy skin, or swelling of the lips, tongue or face), along with at least one of the following: - a high-pitched sound when breathing (stridor) or difficulty breathing. - bluish or purplish lips or fingers. - feeling weak or dizzy, or loss of consciousness. - a very rapid heartbeat. - stomach ache, vomiting or diarrhea (watery stools). what you can do at home what to do if it is a severe allergic reaction, call 911 immediately. inject epinephrine (e.g., epipen, allerject or emerade) as soon as possible, if available. do not drive yourself to the emergency department. ask someone else to drive. how to use epinephrine (e.g., epipen, allerject or emerade) follow the instructions provided by the pharmacist or those included with the epinephrine. give a second dose of epinephrine after 10 minutes if symptoms continue. while waiting for emergency services loosen tight clothing. lie down and raise your legs. sit up if you have difficulty breathing. lie on your side if vomiting occurs. avoid sudden movements. for young children: help them stay calm and reassure them. for pregnant people: lie on the left side. when to go to the hospital right away after receiving epinephrine (e.g., epipen, allerject or emerade): even if symptoms improve or go away even if the injection was given by accident after contact with a food or substance that has previously caused a severe allergic reaction need help? call info-santé at 811, option 1, to speak with a nurse at any time. go to the pharmacy for advice on medications or treatment. make an appointment at your medical clinic or clsc. if you do not have a family doctor, go to gap.soinsvirtuels or call 811, option 3.
