health problems and deaths. it can have direct effects on health, such as frostbite and hypothermia. the cold can also have an indirect effect on health, by aggravating certain illnesses. carbon monoxide poisoning is another indirect effect of the cold on health. aggravation of pre-existing illnesses official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-effects-of-cold-temperatures-on-health cold weather can aggravate certain respiratory illnesses, such as asthma, chronic bronchitis and emphysema especially when these are poorly controlled. the health of people with cardiovascular illnesses can also worsen due to cold temperatures. people with angina or those who have already suffered a heart attack or a stroke can experience a worsening of symptoms. carbon monoxide poisoning official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-effects-of-cold-temperatures-on-health during cold weather, some people use space heaters to keep warm at home. also, during power outages, many people use appliances that burn fuel, such as propane, wood or oil. if they are not designed for indoor use or are not in good condition, these devices should not be used inside the house or in enclosed spaces (garage, shed, workshop, etc.) because they can release carbon monoxide. be sure to place them at a safe distance from the house, away from air vents. carbon monoxide detectors should be installed throughout the house. carbon monoxide is an invisible and odourless gas that can cause potentially fatal poisoning. for further information, consult the carbon monoxide poisoning page. protection and prevention official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-effects-of-cold-temperatures-on-health you can take certain precautions to prevent experiencing the effects of cold temperatures. you will find advice on how to protect yourself and your loved ones during periods of extreme cold on the preventing the harmful effects of cold temperatures page. people at risk official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-effects-of-cold-temperatures-on-health
