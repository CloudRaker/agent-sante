roseola (sixth disease) (https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/roseola-sixth-disease/) official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/roseola-sixth-disease/ roseola infantum what it is roseola is a contagious viral illness that mainly affects children under 4 years of age. it is usually harmless and does not always have symptoms. the main symptoms are fever, runny nose, sore throat, and irritability. small, non-itchy pink spots may also appear on the body. quoi faire à la maison à faire drink small amounts of cool water often, unless a healthcare professional advises otherwise. take acetaminophen (tylenol) or ibuprofen (advil). when in doubt, ask a pharmacist for advice. important: ibuprofen is contraindicated for children under 6 months of age. to prevent spreading the infection wash hands often. do not share items that have been in contact with the sick person's mouth. stay home during the most contagious period. avoid close contact with other people, especially babies, children, older adults, and people who are immunocompromised. regularly clean and disinfect frequently touched surfaces and objects.
