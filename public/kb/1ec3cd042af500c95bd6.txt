official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/cancer/cancer-treatment drugs are an integral part of cancer treatment. they are classified into several categories: chemotherapy, which aims to destroy fast-growing cells, such as cancer cells immunotherapy, which stimulates the immune system to destroy cancer cells or prevent them from spreading hormone therapy, which acts on hormone production in order to slow down cancer cells whose development depends on these hormones targeted therapies, which specifically attack the molecular abnormalities (proteins, genes) of cancer cells
