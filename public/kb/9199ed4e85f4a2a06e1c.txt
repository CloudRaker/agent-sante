legal framework (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/blood-tissue-and-organ-donation/compensation-plan-for-victims-of-a-hema-quebec-product/legal-framework) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/blood-tissue-and-organ-donation/compensation-plan-for-victims-of-a-hema-quebec-product/legal-framework the act respecting héma-québec and the biovigilance committee ensures a legal framework for héma-québec activities. it includes a chapter (articles 54.1 to 54.12) which provides compensation for victims of a product distributed by héma-québec. the regulation respecting the conditions for compensation to victims of a héma-québec product stipulates elements of the compensation plan, especially eligibility criteria and the terms of a compensation claim. last update: may 6, 2019
