sore throat official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/sore-throat/ what it is official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/sore-throat/ a sore throat causes pain, scratchiness or a burning feeling in the throat. swallowing or speaking may be difficult for a few days. what you can do at home official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/sore-throat/ what to do for adults and children who can gargle: gargle with warm salt water for about 1 minute. mix together: 240 ml (1 cup or 8 oz.) of safe drinking water or boiled water that has been left to cool 2.5 ml (1⁄2 tsp.) of salt for children aged 4 years and older: let an ice cube or a lozenge (cough drop) dissolve slowly in the mouth. to relieve pain, take acetaminophen (tylenol) or ibuprofen (advil). when in doubt, ask a pharmacist for advice. important: ibuprofen is contraindicated for children under 6 months of age. what to eat drink warm or cold fluids, whichever feels more soothing. eat soft foods that are easy to swallow, such as soup, puréed foods, eggs, cottage cheese, or yogurt. try cold foods, such as ice pops (popsicles) or frozen yogurt. avoid smoking. places where there is smoke or dust. citrus juices, such as orange, lemon, or grapefruit juice, which may irritate the throat. alcohol. when to seek medical attention official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/sore-throat/ fever, sore throat and difficulty swallowing after contact with someone who has a strep throat infection: get tested pain lasting more than 3 days with no improvement fever that does not go down despite medication skin that changes colour or becomes very rough, like sandpaper for people aged 75 years and older: difficulty carrying out one or more activities of daily living, such as preparing meals or bathing need help?
