respecting the governance of the health and social services system (act or lgssss) recognizes the right of english-speakers to receive health and social services in english, in keeping with the program to health and social services intended for the english-speaking population. this program: identifies the services offered by institutions that are accessible in english for english-speaking persons may, with the agreement of a private institution, indicate the services that may be provided in english to santé québec users by that institution under an agreement the access program must be reviewed every five years and remains valid until a new program is approved. services offered in english by institutions and facilities official source:
