available to offer them warm support, listen to them and help them find solutions. mental health young people who feel overwhelmed by negative emotions or thoughts can get help at an aire ouverte centre. there is no need to have a mental health diagnosis to get support. a few examples of situations where an aire ouverte centre can help: anxiety and stress depression suicide prevention self-harm low self-esteem insomnia concerns about the mental health of a loved one sexual health an aire ouverte centre offers sexual health services, in particular to obtain or learn about: contraception, screening sexual and gender identity pregnancy tests terminations of pregnancy condoms healthy romantic and sexual relationships substance use an aire ouverte centre can help and support young people who are questioning or worried about their: use of cigarettes, including vaping drinking drug use gambling screen or internet use, etc. relationship issues the aire ouverte team can help young people who are going through conflicts (family, romantic partners, etc.), whether violent or not, or who are experiencing bullying. other needs at an aire ouverte centre, young people will find support for any kind of need other than those mentioned above. for example, for all kinds of questions, for any distress or concerns for themselves or for a loved one, the aire ouverte team will be able to meet their needs. services and opening hours may vary from one site to another. consult the information sheets for each aire ouverte centre for details and contact information. find an their families and friends, can get support and services adapted to their needs: in person, by telephone or virtually walk-in or appointment basis at the place of their choice in all regions of québec aire ouverte services are available on a flexible schedule and without waitlist. aire ouverte opening hours are adjusted to suit the hours young people are available. teleconsultation services and appointment scheduling by email or text message are generally offered. aire ouverte centres are often located in places that are easy to get to on foot, by public transport or by car. in addition, each aire ouverte centre is accessible to everyone: young people can go to the aire ouverte centre of their choice, regardless of where they come from. the aire ouverte team is available to listen and help find solutions, without judgment and regardless of the person's situation, occupation or condition. aire ouverte centres in deployment other aire ouverte centres are currently being developed.
