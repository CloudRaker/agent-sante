common cold official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/common-cold/ what is it official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/common-cold/ the common cold is a viral infection of the upper respiratory tract, including the nose, throat, and sometimes the sinuses. the most common symptoms are a runny nose, cough, and sore throat. what you can do at home official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/common-cold/ what to do get as much rest as possible. sleep with the head slightly elevated - for example, by placing a pillow under the mattress or slightly raising the head of the bed. take warm showers or baths. the steam may make breathing easier. for children: let them play in the bath, or stay with them in the shower. keep the air in the home clean and slightly humid, at 30% to 50% humidity. for children aged 4 years and older: offer sugar-free hard candy or honey lozenges (cough drops). ask a pharmacist to recommend the right medication to relieve symptoms. to relieve pain, take acetaminophen (tylenol) or ibuprofen (advil). when in doubt, ask a pharmacist for advice. important: ibuprofen is contraindicated for children under 6 months of age. to stay hydrated and nourished drink plenty of water. drink warm fluids, such as broth. eat several small meals, even when appetite is reduced. for babies and young children: offer the breast, bottle or other fluids often. to relieve other symptoms if needed, see the health advice sheets on: cough stuffy nose (nasal congestion) sore throat to prevent spreading the infection wash hands often. do not share objects that have been in contact with the person who is sick. stay home during the most contagious period. avoid close contact with others, especially babies, children, older adults and people who are immunocompromised. regularly clean and disinfect frequently touched surfaces and objects. avoid smoking or vaping places where there is smoke or dust when to seek medical attention official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/common-cold/ fever that continues to worsen despite following the recommended advice fever that begins after 3 days of cold symptoms despite following the recommended advice sore throat, cough or runny nose lasting more than 7 days with no improvement cough lasting more than 3 weeks despite following the recommended advice ear pain that persists despite following the recommended advice. see the health advice sheets on: middle ear infection outer ear infection fever in people at higher risk, including people with weakened immune systems, people who are pregnant and babies under 3 months of age for people aged 75 years and older: fever and difficulty carrying out one or more activities of daily living, such as preparing meals or bathing need help? call info-santé at 811, option 1, to speak with a nurse at any time. go to the pharmacy for advice on medications or treatment. make an appointment at your medical clinic or clsc. if you do not have a family doctor, go to gap.soinsvirtuels or call 811, option 3. when to go to the hospital right away official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/common-cold/
