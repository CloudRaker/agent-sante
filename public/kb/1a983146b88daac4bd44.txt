you are a flight risk; you could end up in a dangerous situation while you are on the run; your behaviour or actions could be dangerous for someone else while you are on the run; based on a review of your situation, your placement in an intensive supervision unit is not justified. why are steps being taken to keep me from running away? official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2020/20-838-02a.pdf for your own safety. we don't want you to end up in a situation that could be dangerous for you or where you could behave inappropriately toward someone else. for instance, we want to help you avoid situations where you could be sexually exploited, abuse drugs or alcohol, or behave in a violent or threatening manner toward someone else. who makes the decision to take measures to keep me from running away? official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2020/20-838-02a.pdf after reviewing your situation and discussing it with you and your parents, your youth care worker and social worker may recommend that measures be taken to keep you from running away. the executive director of the integrated centre, or the person the executive director authorizes in writing, makes the decision as to whether measures should be taken to keep you from running away. their decision is based on an analysis of your situation. the executive director or the person they authorize will also make sure your situation does not justify your placement in an intensive supervision unit. you and your parents will be informed of the reasons justifying the decision to take measures to keep you from running away. measures to keep you from running away cannot be used to punish or isolate you. however: official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2020/20-838-02a.pdf if you do not respect the rules of the unit, you could be subject to disciplinary measures such as losing privileges; and if you put your safety or that of others at risk, you could be isolated temporarily to keep you from hurting yourself or someone else, and you will remain isolated for as long as you remain a threat. what happens while measures are being taken to keep me from running away? official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2020/20-838-02a.pdf your youth care worker will help you through the process, so you give up on running away. they will work with you to figure out why you want to run away, help you find other solutions to your problem, or help you find ways to deal with the difficulties you're facing. the goal is to put an end to the situation and keep it from happening again in the short-term. the team of youth care workers will also be there to give you the support and guidance you need. you can keep up with your studies and you will have access to the activities and services offered within the rehabilitation centre. one of the measures taken to keep you from running away may be to restrict your freedom to leave your unit or the rehabilitation centre. your activities may also have to be supervised or rescheduled. when will the measures to keep me from running away be lifted? official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2020/20-838-02a.pdf they will be lifted once it is determined that you are no longer a flight risk or the situation that justified them is unlikely to recur in the days to come. your situation may be reassessed at any time, but no later than 7 days after the measures are implemented. it is the executive director of the integrated centre, or the person the executive director authorizes in writing, who will reassess your situation and decide whether to: put an end to the measures keeping you from running away; the term "integrated centre" refers to both integrated health and social services centres (cisss) and integrated university health and social services centres (ciusss). maintain the measures keeping you from running away and reassess your situation within 7 days; or place you in an intensive supervision unit.
