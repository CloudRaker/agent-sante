looking for a family doctor? (https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2018/18-949-01a.pdf) official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2018/18-949-01a.pdf looking for a family doctor? québec.ca/gamf put your name on the waiting list to find a family doctor: x register online at québec.ca/gamf x register via phone at 1-877-644-4545 (to get the number specific to your area) to register, you must: x not already be registered with a family doctor x have a valid medical insurance card x reside at the address in your régie de l'assurance maladie du québec file x live in québec x be 14 years of age or older. a person under the age of 14 must be registered by someone who has reached the age of eligibility. québec.ca/gamf
