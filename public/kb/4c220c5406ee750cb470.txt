how to get advice about a non-urgent psychosocial problem (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/primary-care-health-and-social-services/how-get-advice-about-non-urgent-psychosocial-problem) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/service-organization/primary-care-health-and-social-services/how-get-advice-about-non-urgent-psychosocial-problem contact info-social at 811 (option 2) for advice regarding a non-urgent psychosocial problem, you can contact info-social by calling 811 (option 2). a psychosocial worker will answer your questions, make recommendations and, if necessary, refer you to an appropriate resource in your territory. the service is available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. for example, you can call info-social if: you are worried or anxious about a situation or a behaviour; you are having difficulties with your family or with your partner. last update: february 23, 2023
