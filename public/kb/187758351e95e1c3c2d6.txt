technical walking aid program official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/technical-walking-aid-program description official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/technical-walking-aid-program through this program, you can obtain a loaner walker. walkers consist of a light metal frame, four wheels, handles connected to a braking system, a seat with backrest, a basket, and a folding mechanism. presentation and objectives official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/technical-walking-aid-program walkers make it easier to travel over short and medium distances. this program consists of providing walkers to people with reduced mobility to promote their social integration and make it easier for them to stay in their living environment. allocation of these technical aids is based on an overall assessment of the person's needs and resources. eligibility official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/technical-walking-aid-program to be eligible for this program, you must: live in québec; have a physical impairment that prevents you from walking distances or a severe; respiratory or cardiovascular impairment; be of the age of majority; have sufficient use of your upper limbs to use the brakes safely; be able to use a walker on your own. you are not eligible if you: already benefit from the same type of assistance provided by another national or federal program or a program offered by private insurers; have severe balance issues; have another government-provided mobility aid, such as a wheelchair, scooter, or service dog that only functions as a walking aid; live in a residential long-term care centre or a public intermediate resource. reimbursable expenses official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/technical-walking-aid-program there are no fees or deductibles to pay. for those who have private insurance coverage or are on social assistance, this program can be used to pay the uncovered portion of the costs. all equipment allocated must be insured by the user. it must be returned when the person is no longer using it or if he or she dies. process official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/technical-walking-aid-program to enrol in this program, contact your territorial establishment. for contact information, consult the following page: find your health and social services facility. you will need to provide a certificate relating to the physical impairment or severe cardiorespiratory impairment, completed by a doctor or a specialized nurse practitioner. help and information official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/technical-walking-aid-program for assistance, contact the office des personnes handicapées du québec. see also official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/technical-walking-aid-program mobility assistance dog reimbursement program mobility scooter program assistive devices for disability or asd devices that compensate for a physical deficiency last update: july 22, 2024
