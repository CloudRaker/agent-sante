healthcare network and people affected by cancer. every person affected by cancer develops knowledge as a result of their experience. this knowledge comes from everything they went through during the developed quality assessment and improvement in the centres where they receive care and services the organization of care and services by participating in coordination committees in healthcare institutions the goal is to find out and integrate their perspective in order to build a better cancer care network. oncology nurse navigator official source:
