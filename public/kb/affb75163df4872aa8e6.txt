promotional material and vaccination reminders (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry/promotional-material-and-vaccination-reminders) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/your-health-information/quebec-vaccination-registry/promotional-material-and-vaccination-reminders your territorial establishment may contact you or send you promotional material on vaccines. they will also be able to contact you if you must receive other doses of a vaccine. the vaccines you should get are based on the regular vaccination schedule and the vaccines you have already received. refusal to receive promotional material or vaccination reminders at any time, you may refuse to have a territorial establishment send you: vaccination promotional material reminders of vaccines that you should get for your protection your refusal can be permanent or temporary. you can modify your decision at any time. to express your refusal you can express your refusal by completing the refusal form or modification of refusal form. once completed, mail it to the address indicated on the form. make sure that you provide your health insurance number. you can contact your territorial establishment to obtain a paper version of the form. to cancel your refusal you can modify your decision by completing another refusal form or modification of refusal form. once completed, mail it to the address indicated on the form. make sure that you provide your health insurance number. you can contact your territorial establishment to obtain a paper version of the form. last update: april 20, 2018
