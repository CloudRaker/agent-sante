https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/accueil/about.html a single requirement: having a québec health insurance card. persons not eligible for the québec health insurance plan (for instance, persons having been outside québec for an extended period) or those whose health insurance card is expired can still schedule an appointment. they will be notified, however, that they must normalize their situation with ramq before attending their appointment. scheduling an appointment in 3 easy steps official source: https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/accueil/about.html 1 enter your personal information fill out the 6 and time enter the desired date and time of day, as well as the reason for your consultation to obtain an appointment. the québec medical appointment scheduler offers several appointment options. select the desired time-slot among those displayed by clicking on it. 3 confirm the appointment click on the "confirm the appointment button". a confirmation of the date, time and location of the appointment, along with the reason for the consultation will be displayed on the screen, as will a unique reference number. this number is necessary to cancel an appointment, consult the information about the appointment or update the contact information entered for the appointment. depending on the means of communication selected when you scheduled the appointment, the confirmation and reminders will be sent by email, automated telephone call or text message. advantages official source: https://www.rvsq.gouv.qc.ca/accueil/about.html free secure available 24/7 simplified access to a health
