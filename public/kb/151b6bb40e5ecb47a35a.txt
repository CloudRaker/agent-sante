the health effects of air pollution official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-health-effects-of-air-pollution description official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-health-effects-of-air-pollution when air quality is poor or when a smog warning (yellowish fog comprising gas and particles) is in effect, it means that there is a high amount of pollutants in the air. the main outdoor air pollutants are: fine particles (pm2.5), which are tiny particles that can penetrate deeply into the respiratory system ozone (o3) sulphur dioxide (so2) nitrogen dioxide (no2) carbon monoxide (co) among other sources, these pollutants come from: motor vehicles factories heating systems (oil, propane, wood, etc.) forest fires air pollutants can affect the entire population's health. some people, however, are more sensitive or more exposed than others to pollutants and more likely to experience adverse effects. symptoms official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-health-effects-of-air-pollution people who breathe polluted air can develop a variety of symptoms. these vary according to each person's sensitivity to air pollution. the main short-term symptoms are: eye irritation wheezing or difficulty breathing irritation and inflammation of the respiratory tract (coughing or sputum) shortness of breath, especially during physical activity aggravation of existing asthma, heart and lung conditions the severity of these symptoms depends on a person's health and the level of pollution to which he or she is exposed. the improper use of certain medications can make symptoms of pollution worse. it is important to note that symptoms can be the result of factors other than air pollution. these other factors include: oppressive heat humidity viruses pollens, such as ragweed when to consult official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-health-effects-of-air-pollution you should consult a healthcare professional as soon as possible if you have any of the following symptoms: chest tightness pain when breathing deeply difficulty breathing despite not having engaged in physical activity if you have questions regarding your health, call info-santé 811. complications official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-health-effects-of-air-pollution people who breathe polluted air regularly over several years can develop certain health problems: chronic lung diseases, such as chronic bronchitis cardiovascular diseases, meaning the heart and blood vessels disorders lung cancer breathing polluted air can also worsen symptoms for people already suffering from chronic illnesses, especially respiratory or cardiac diseases. in rare cases, this can lead to hospitalization or premature death. risk factors official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-health-effects-of-air-pollution the risks of experiencing discomfort due to air pollution increase: when outdoor air quality is poor when a smog warning is in effect when the heat is oppressive during strenuous physical activity or long outings, when people breathe a large amount of air people at risk official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-health-effects-of-air-pollution some people are more at risk of showing symptoms if exposed to poor quality air or smog: people with respiratory problems, such as: asthma emphysema chronic bronchitis other chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, etc. people with heart disease, such as angina people who have had a heart attack babies and children younger than 5 years of age people with a chronic illness, such as diabetes, heart disease, especially if uncontrolled (angina, previous heart attack, diabetes, etc.); pregnant people seniors people who do intense outdoor exercise or who work outdoors regularly. these people can breathe a large quantity of polluted air protection and prevention official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/advice-and-prevention/health-and-environment/the-health-effects-of-air-pollution get into the habit of verifying the air quality in your region. when it is poor or a smog warning is in effect, you can take certain precautions. read preventing the health effects of air pollution, which offers information on how to ensure your well-being
