program regarding bandages and compression garments used to treat lymphedema (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/lymphedema/program-regarding-bandages-and-compression-garments-used-to-treat-lymphedema) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/lymphedema/program-regarding-bandages-and-compression-garments-used-to-treat-lymphedema the program regarding bandages and compression garments used to treat lymphedema allows a portion of the fees of these products to be reimbursed. eligibility all persons with quebec public health insurance and suffering from primary or secondary lymphedema are eligible for this program. reimbursement of costs annual reimbursement covers a portion of the cost for supplies, such as multi-layer bandages and compression garments. to know all eligible fees, check out the régie de l'assurance maladie du québec (ramq)'s compression garments for lymphedema page. procedure the ramq manages this program on behalf of the ministère de la santé et des services sociaux (msss). to learn about steps to take in sending your reimbursement request and to download forms, go to the ramq's compression garments for lymphedema page. see also lymphedema last update: december 20, 2018
