typhoid (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/typhoid) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-issues/a-z/typhoid transmission typhoid is spread by contact with water or food contaminated by the stools of a person with typhoid. signs and symptoms fever headache abdominal pain constipation slow pulse loss of appetite possible complication death (less than 1% of cases even with treatment) available vaccines injectable typhoid vaccine oral typhoid vaccine last update: june 22, 2023
