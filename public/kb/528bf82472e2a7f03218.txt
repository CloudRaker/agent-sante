numbness (https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/numbness/) official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/numbness/ what it is numbness is when a part of the body, such as an arm or a leg, loses some feeling. it may tingle or become harder to move. what you can do at home what to do try and move the numb body part. change position. take breaks and stretch often when making the same movements repeatedly. what to avoid be careful not to injure the numb body part (it may be difficult to feel pain). when to seek medical attention numbness that starts gradually and gets worse recent surgery or trauma tingling or severe pain in the hand or arm at night pregnant people: always seek medical attention if numbness occurs. need help? call info-santé at 811, option 1, to speak with a nurse at any time. go to the pharmacy for advice on medications or treatment. make an appointment at your medical clinic or clsc. if you do not have a family doctor, go to gap.soinsvirtuels or call 811, option 3. when to go to the hospital right away rapid change in overall condition: unusual tiredness (drowsiness) or difficulty staying awake less responsive to what is happening around them confusion, agitation, or unusual behaviour reduced appetite and thirst difficulty carrying out usual daily tasks severe headache that comes suddenly very severe chest pain sudden loss of strength or numbness in the face, an arm, or a leg (even if it goes away) difficulty speaking or understanding words perte d'équilibre qui apparaît tout d'un coup avec de la faiblesse, un mal de tête ou de la difficulté à parler ou à voir sudden loss of balance along with weakness, a headache, difficulty speaking, or difficulty seeing fingers or toes turning blue a painful body part that becomes swollen or warm being unable to hold urine (pee)
