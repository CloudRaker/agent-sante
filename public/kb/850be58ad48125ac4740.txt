québec chsld dental care and daily hygiene program - services for residents (https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2019/19-231-27a.pdf) official source: https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/fichiers/2019/19-231-27a.pdf services for residents what is the québec chsld dental care and daily oral hygiene program? loss of independence among the elderly often involves a decline in the ability to perform certain tasks, such as brushing teeth and cleaning dentures. this leads to a rapid deterioration in dental and overall health. the program aims to improve and maintain the dental health of people living in residential and long-term care centres (chslds). what are the objectives of the program? where is dental care provided? the program is intended to keep residents comfortable and healthy by ensuring that they: " have good oral hygiene " have no untreated pain or oral disease dental care is tailored to the needs of residents. it is provided on site, in a room reserved for this purpose or in residents' rooms, using portable equipment. " are able to eat painlessly and enjoy eating what dental services are available in chslds? the following dental services are available free of charge for all residents: " oral evaluations on admission and periodically (every six months) thereafter by a nurse working with a nursing assistant as needed. " daily oral hygiene care by an orderly , with support from a nursing assistant as needed (oral hygiene products are provided free of charge by the care centre): tooth brushing denture cleaning " preventive care by a dental hygienist : scaling (cleaning of teeth) labelling dentures with residents' names applying fluoride varnish " exams by a dentist and emergency exams as needed " exams and evaluations by a denturist as needed the cost of the following dental services is covered, in part, by residents, depending on their ability to pay. residents or their families must provide consent before the start of treatment. " services provided by a dentist : fillings extractions root canals making and repairing dentures " services provided by a denturist : making and repairing dentures for more information on the program, please contact the care centre manager or go to québec.ca .
