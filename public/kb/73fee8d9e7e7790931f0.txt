feeling of stiffness or discomfort in the back. it can occur after physical exertion, after staying in an awkward position for a long time, after a fall, or for no obvious reason. the pain can be very severe and spread down into the legs. related sheet available: back pain what you can do at home official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/back-pain/ what to do rest your back. lie down only to sleep. maintain good, ergonomic posture during daily activities. continue physical activity based on how the pain progresses. choose activities such as walking, cycling, or swimming (no jumping). alternate between comfortable positions during daily activities when sitting: rest your back firmly against the backrest. place your arms on the armrests. put your feet on a stool so that your knees are higher than your hips. stay in this position for 20 to 50 minutes at most. lie on your back, with a cushion under your head and another under your knees. lie on your side with your knees bent, with a cushion under your head and another between your knees. lie on your back on the floor, in a position that is comfortable for the lower back: rest your legs on a chair so that your hips and knees are bent at about 90 degrees. place a pillow under your head and another under your legs. stay in this position for 5 to 25 minutes. relieving pain apply a cold compress for 15 to 20 minutes every 2 to 3 hours. use a cold compress, such as, an ice pack, a bag of frozen vegetables, or a cold gel pack. always place a cloth between the skin and the cold source to protect the skin. to reduce pain, take acetaminophen (tylenol) or ibuprofen (advil). when in doubt, ask a pharmacist for advice. important: ibuprofen is contraindicated for children under 6 months of age. à éviter avoid
