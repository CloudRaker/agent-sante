a birth certificate. for a person under 14 years of age, a valid form of identification for the parent or guardian must also be provided. by mail to receive a copy of your vaccination record by mail, allow approximately 20 calendar days for processing. processing times may vary depending on the volume of requests received. no acknowledgment of receipt is sent when a request is received. complete and sign the access to information on vaccines request form. if you do not have a health insurance number issued by the régie de l'assurance maladie du québec (ramq), it is not necessary to provide one. include a photocopy of a valid form of identification, such as: a health insurance card;
