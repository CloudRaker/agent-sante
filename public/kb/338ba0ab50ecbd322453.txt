schedule includes administration of 1 dose at the age of 12 and 18 months. protection against these diseases lasts throughout life. some symptoms may be caused by the vaccine (e.g. redness at the injection site). other problems may occur by chance and are not related to the vaccine (e.g. cold, gastro, headache). mmr-var vaccine is safe. most reactions are harmless and do not last long.
