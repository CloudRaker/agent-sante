pollen allergy (https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/pollen-allergy/) official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/pollen-allergy/ what it is it happens when the body reacts to pollen in the air. it can cause a runny nose, sneezing, breathing problems, eye irritation, and skin reactions. what you can do at home what to do keep doors and windows closed. go outside after it rains, if possible. wash your hair and clothes after spending time outdoors. ask a pharmacist whether over-the-counter medications may help. what to avoid going outside when it is very hot and windy. contact with plants that cause allergies (e.g., ragweed). using air conditioning systems that bring in outdoor air. drying laundry outdoors. mowing the lawn or raking leaves. when to seek medical attention ear pain that persists or gets worse a persistent fever yellow nasal discharge that does not improve after 3 days allergy symptoms that do not improve after 7 days: headache or pain around the eyes tooth pain need help? call info-santé at 811, option 1, to speak with a nurse at any time. go to the pharmacy for advice on medications or treatment. make an appointment at your medical clinic or clsc. if you do not have a family doctor, go to gap.soinsvirtuels or call 811, option 3 when to go to the hospital right away difficulty breathing, especially if: breathing is becoming increasingly difficult, even at rest. there is a high-pitched sound when breathing (stridor). the stomach or chest pulls in with each breath (retractions). fever in people who are more vulnerable to complications: people with a weakened immune system (immunocompromised). pregnant people. infants under 3 months old.
