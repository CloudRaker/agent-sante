infection is contagious and usually causes a sore throat. it may also cause severe fatigue, headache, nausea, and fever. scarlet fever may cause a red rash with small bumps and a red tongue that looks like a strawberry. what you can do at home official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/strep-throat/ what to do get as much rest as possible. replace the toothbrush 24 hours after starting antibiotics. to relieve pain, take acetaminophen (tylenol) or ibuprofen (advil). when in doubt, ask a pharmacist for advice. important: ibuprofen is contraindicated for children under 6 months of age. to relieve a sore throat for adults and children who can gargle: gargle with warm salt water for about 1 minute, several times a day. mix 240 ml (1 cup or 8 oz.) of safe drinking water or boiled water that has been left to cool with 2.5 ml (1⁄2 tsp.) of salt. drink warm or cold fluids, whichever feels more soothing. eat soft foods that are easy to swallow, such as soup, puréed foods, eggs, cottage cheese or yogurt. try something cold, such as popsicles (ice pops) or frozen yogurt. for children aged 4 years and older: let an ice cube or a lozenge (cough drop) dissolve slowly in the mouth. for babies and young children: offer warm or cold water or milk, according to preference. to prevent spreading the infection stay home for the first 24 hours after starting antibiotics. wash hands often. do not share objects that have been in contact with the sick person's mouth. regularly clean and disinfect frequently touched surfaces and objects. avoid close contact with others, especially babies, children, older adults and people who are immunocompromised. avoid smoking or vaping places where there is smoke or dust when to seek medical attention official source:
