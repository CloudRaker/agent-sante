stiffness or discomfort in the neck. it can occur after staying in an awkward position for a long time, after a sudden awkward movement, or for no clear reason. it can be very painful and spread to the shoulders or arms. related sheet available: back pain what you can do at home official source: https://sante.quebec/en/health-advice/neck-pain/ what to do avoid straining the neck. sleep on your back, keeping your head aligned with the body. place a pillow under your head and under your knees. maintain good posture during daily activities. when sitting, rest your back against the backrest and place your arms on the armrests. avoid sitting for long periods of time without moving. when sitting for a long time is necessary, gently move the neck and take short walks from time to time. continue physical activity based on how the pain progresses. relieving pain apply a hot or cold compress, whichever feels better. always place a cloth between the skin and the heat or cold source to protect the skin. apply a cold compress for 15 to 20 minutes every 2 to 3 hours. use a cold compress, such as, an ice pack, a bag of frozen vegetables, or a cold gel pack. or apply a hot compress for 20 to 30 minutes, 4 times a day. use a damp towel warmed in the microwave, a warm compress, or a hot water bottle. to reduce pain, take acetaminophen (tylenol) or ibuprofen (advil). when in doubt, ask a pharmacist for advice. important: ibuprofen is contraindicated for children under 6 months of age. avoid do not wear a cervical collar unless advised by a healthcare professional. avoid sleeping on
