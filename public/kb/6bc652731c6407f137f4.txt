eligibility criteria official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource/quebec-family-doctor-finder to put your name on the waiting list to find a family doctor using the québec family doctor finder (gamf), you must meet the following eligibility criteria: you must have a valid health insurance card. you must not already be registered with a family doctor or a snppc. however, if your doctor or your snppc can no longer see you or if you wish to change doctor or snppc, see the section registering to the waiting list to find a family doctor of the page registering with a family doctor or a specialized nurse practitioner in primary care. you must live at the address indicated in your file at the régie de l'assurance maladie du québec (ramq). if you do not live at that address, you can make a change of address. you must be 14 years of age or over. if you are under 14 years of age, someone else who is 14 years of age or over must put your name on the waiting list on your behalf. you must live in québec and your postal code must be listed by canada post. doctors and snppcs in the nunavik and cree territory of james bay regions cannot register patients. if you live health. it is impossible to determine exactly how long you will be on the waiting list. the waiting time can vary depending on the availability of doctors and snppcs in your region and the number of people on the waiting list for your territory. updating a registration with the québec family doctor finder official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/finding-a-resource/quebec-family-doctor-finder do not forget to update your phone numbers and your email so that you can be reached easily once a doctor, a snppc or a (gamf); online, through the québec health booklet; by telephone, by contacting your territory waiting list (guichet d'accès pour la clientèle orpheline - gaco). to change your address, you must use the service québécois de changement d'adresse (sqca). in addition to being forwarded to several departments and agencies, including the régie de l'assurance maladie du québec, your new address will be automatically updated in the gamf. by keeping your registration up to date, you will keep your place on the waiting list. reporting a change in your health condition official source:
