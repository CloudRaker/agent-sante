health benefits of fish (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/nutrition/healthy-eating-habits/fish-consumption-and-health/health-benefits-of-fish-1) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/nutrition/healthy-eating-habits/fish-consumption-and-health/health-benefits-of-fish-1 fish contains many nutrients. it is: rich in minerals, such as selenium and iodine an excellent source of protein the best food source of natural vitamin d the best source of omega-3 fatty acids. these fatty acids: are essential for the body's development and proper function, especially the brain and retina are essential for a healthy pregnancy would protect against cardiovascular disease would protect the brain against diseases such as dementia, alzheimer, and age-related memory loss have anti-inflammatory effects fish is also low in saturated fat and cholesterol. frequent consumption of fatty fish high in omega-3, such as salmon, sardines, trout and atlantic mackerel, is said to have a protective effect against age-related macular degeneration, the leading cause of vision loss in seniors. see also potential risks of eating fish fish consumption recommendations last update: november 19, 2025
