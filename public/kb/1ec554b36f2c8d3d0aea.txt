ostomy appliances program (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/ostomy-appliances-program) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/ostomy-appliances-program this program provides a lump-sum payment covering, in full or in part, the cost of appliances and supplies for persons having undergone a permanent or temporary ostomy (colostomy, ileostomy or urostomy. a stoma is a surgically created opening made in the abdomen to allow waste materials to be transported out of the body. the program allows for an annual lump-sum payment for the purchase of appliances and supplies (bags and other products) and their replacement. obtain more information on the program last update: december 19, 2025
