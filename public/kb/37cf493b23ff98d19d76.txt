screening centres (cdd) are medical imaging centres designated by the ministère de la santé et des services sociaux to provide screening mammograms. cdd can be medical imaging laboratories or hospital centres. in addition to mammograms, many cdd provide free additional examinations. you may have to pay for some additional tests given in medical imaging laboratories. only centres designated by the ministère de la santé et des services sociaux can provide mammograms under the pqdcs. to be designated, clinics and hospital centres are subjected to rigorous quality standards. they must meet the following requirements: be accredited by the canadian association of radiologists' mammography accreditation program. this accreditation must be renewed every 3 years. hold certification in mammography from the institut national de santé publique du québec's laboratoire de santé publique du québec (in french only). this certification attests, among other things, that mammography devices are safe. it must be renewed annually. be recommended by the respective region's establishment. territorial establishments are responsible for recommending to the ministère clinics and hospital centres that should be designated cdd in their territory. all practitioners working in these centres must have the skills required by their professional associations. in québec, professional associations ensure the quality of their members' professional actions. to find contact information for cdd in your region, consult the following section. to find out about wait times by cdd, consult
