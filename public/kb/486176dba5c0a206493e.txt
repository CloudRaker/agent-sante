visual devices program (https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/visual-devices-program) official source: https://www.quebec.ca/en/health/health-system-and-services/assistive-devices-disabilities-and-handicaps/visual-devices-program this program allows you to obtain visual aids and, if you qualify, receive an amount to take care of a guide dog. the visual aids that are loaned to you compensate for a partial or total loss of vision and support you in your daily activities. the program covers the loan, replacement and repair of visual aids. examples of loaned aids: reading: video magnifier, optical system, calculator writing: brailler mobility: white cane, electronic obstacle detector, night vision goggles obtain more information on the program last update: february 23, 2023
